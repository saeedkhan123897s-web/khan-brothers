Privacy Policy

Welcome to Khan Brothers Motorcycle Workshop & Spare Parts.

This Privacy Policy explains how customer information may be collected, used and managed when using our website and services.

Last Updated: To Be Confirmed

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when customers use our website or services:

  • Customer Full Name
  • Mobile Phone Number
  • Email Address
  • Delivery Address
  • Motorcycle Brand and Model
  • Order and Purchase Information
  • Workshop Service Information
  • Payment Transaction References

2. How We Use Customer Information

Customer information may be used for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Creating and managing customer accounts.
  • Processing spare parts orders.
  • Managing delivery information.
  • Providing motorcycle workshop services.
  • Confirming product compatibility.
  • Managing payments and invoices.
  • Providing customer support.
  • Sharing important order updates.

3. Information Sharing

Customer information may be shared with authorized service providers when necessary to operate the business.

Examples may include:

  • Delivery and courier companies.
  • Payment processing providers.
  • Website hosting providers.
  • Technical service providers.

Customer information will not be treated as public information or shared without a legitimate business or legal reason.

4. Payment Information

Customers may use supported payment methods when placing online orders.

  • Cash on Delivery.
  • Bank Transfer.
  • Debit and Credit Cards.
  • Supported Digital Wallets.

Where an external payment gateway is used, payment processing will be handled according to that provider's procedures.

Full card numbers, security codes and sensitive card credentials should not be stored directly in our website database.

5. Information Security

We aim to protect customer information through appropriate technical and organizational security measures.

  • Authorized user access.
  • Secure account authentication.
  • Protected database access.
  • Regular data backups.
  • Monitoring of important system activities.

6. Information Retention

Customer records may be retained for as long as necessary to provide services, manage business records and meet applicable legal obligations.

Retention periods and deletion procedures will be established according to the business requirements and applicable laws.

7. Customer Rights

Customers may contact Khan Brothers to request assistance with their personal information.

  • Request access to personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Ask questions about information usage.
  • Request account closure where applicable.

Some records may need to be retained where required for business or legal purposes.

8. Cookies and Website Data

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to support website functionality, user sessions and customer preferences.

Cookie usage will be explained to customers when these technologies are implemented.

9. Third-Party Services

Our website may contain links to external websites or services.

External websites have their own privacy policies and data handling practices. Customers should review those policies before using external services.

10. Changes to Privacy Policy

Khan Brothers may update this Privacy Policy when business operations, technologies or applicable requirements change.

Updated information will be published on this website with the revised date.

11. Contact Us

For questions or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us.

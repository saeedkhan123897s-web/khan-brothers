Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our motorcycle workshop, spare parts, online orders and customer services.

Workshop Services

What motorcycle repair services do you offer?

We list services such as engine repair, oil changes, brake service, electrical repair and chain and sprocket maintenance. Please contact our workshop to confirm available services.

Do I need to book a repair appointment?

Please contact Khan Brothers to confirm workshop availability and appointment arrangements.

Can I get an estimated repair cost?

Yes. You can discuss the motorcycle problem with our workshop and request a repair estimate before approving the repair work.

Can I check my motorcycle repair status?

Workshop job tracking is planned as part of our management system. Contact the workshop for current repair status information.

Spare Parts & Products

Which motorcycle brands do you support?

Our product catalogue is intended to cover different motorcycle brands, including Honda, Yamaha and Suzuki. Please confirm the required part's availability with our shop.

How can I find the correct spare part?

Provide your motorcycle brand, model and year. You can also share the existing part's code or picture to help identify the correct product.

How can I check product availability?

Visit our spare parts catalogue or contact Khan Brothers to confirm current stock availability.

Can I order spare parts online?

Online spare parts ordering is part of our planned website functionality. Actual orders will be accepted once the online store is operational.

Delivery coverage and availability will be confirmed by Khan Brothers before accepting an order.

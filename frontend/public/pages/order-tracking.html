Order Status Information

Your order can pass through the following stages:

  1. Order Placed

    Customer has submitted the order.

  2. Order Confirmed

    The shop has verified the order and product availability.

  3. Processing

    Products are being prepared for shipment.

  4. Dispatched

    The order has been handed over for delivery.

  5. Out for Delivery

    The delivery person is taking the order to the customer.

  6. Delivered

    The customer has received the order.
